# The sidebar entry is missing

## What you see

There is no Floor plan item in the Home Assistant sidebar.

## Why it happens

The sidebar entry is optional and can be switched off in the integration options. The editor is for administrators only, other users do not see it.

## What to do

1. Open **Settings, Devices & services, FNS Floorplan, Configure** and turn the sidebar entry on.
2. Check that you are logged in as an administrator.
3. The editor is always available at `/fns-floorplan`, even without the sidebar entry.
