# Something else does not work

## What you see

Your problem is not described in any of the other articles.

## Why it happens

It may be a bug, or a combination of settings that nobody has met yet.

## What to do

1. Open an issue on GitHub.
2. Write the version (Settings, Devices & services), what you expected and what happened.
3. Attach the browser console log if the card does not load.
