# The plan is empty

## What you see

The card is empty, there are no rooms.

## Why it happens

An empty card means that no plan was saved yet. If you saved before, a save may have been overwritten or lost, the editor keeps the last 20 saves.

## What to do

1. Open **Floor plan** in the sidebar, draw the rooms and press **Save**.
2. If you saved before and the plan is gone, open the **History** in the editor and restore one of the last 20 saves.
