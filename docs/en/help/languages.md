# Some texts are not translated

## What you see

Room names or item texts stay in one language while the rest of the card changes language.

## Why it happens

The card and the editor follow the language of your Home Assistant profile (English, Czech and German). Texts that you wrote yourself, such as room names or `text`, are not translated.

## What to do

1. Change the language in your Home Assistant profile if the interface is in the wrong one.
2. Write your own texts in the language you want to see.
3. The documentation is available in all three languages, use the language switch at the top of the site.
